Transparent Policies

House Rules & Rental Terms

Simple, fair guidelines to ensure harmony, safety, and comfort for all residents.

Rent & Deposit

Monthly rent is due each month. 1-month refundable security deposit is required upon check-in.

30-Day Notice

Minimum stay is 1 month. A 30-day advance notice is required prior to move-out for full deposit refund.

Visitor Policy

Visitors are permitted in common areas until 8:00 PM. Prior warden approval is needed for overnight guests.

Clean & Safe Zone

No smoking inside room premises. Quiet hours begin at 10:30 PM to respect everyone's sleep and study time.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common queries about booking, facilities, and stay policies.

What items are included in the room? ▼
Every room includes a bed with fresh linen, mattress, pillow, individual wardrobe/locker, fan, AC (in AC rooms), and attached/shared washroom access.
Are there any hidden maintenance or electricity costs? ▼
No hidden costs! Monthly rent covers Wi-Fi, housekeeping, in-flat washing machine, security, and maintenance. Electricity is billed as per meter usage in AC rooms.
Can I schedule a physical room visit before booking? ▼
Yes! Click the 'Book a Visit' button on the top right or message us on WhatsApp to pick a convenient visit time.
